Fixtures

Club fixtures, competitions and opportunities to play.

The Club's fixtures range from A-class to beginners' fixtures and all members are encouraged to play. Since many games are played handicap you are guaranteed a good game, whatever your ability. Members are strongly encouraged to get involved, if you are interested please contact the Captain.

This year's fixtures calendar is still being drawn up, but some matches have now been confirmed. Details of these, and some other forthcoming attractions, are given below. Please keep checking back, as we have a number of matches still to be confirmed.
