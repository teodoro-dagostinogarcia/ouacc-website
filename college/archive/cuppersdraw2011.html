Last Draw Update: 19/06/2011 2049 BST

The 2011 seeded teams, based on last years competition, are (in order from 1st seed to 8th seed): St Edmund Hall, New, St Annes,
St Edmund Hall 2, Lincoln, New MCR, New 2, Keble

The deadlines for matches to be played are given in the draw table; remember that University weeks START on a Sunday!
The winning team is responsible for sending the result of the match to the Cuppers Secretary at luke.valori@trinity.ox.ac.uk. If no result
is sent in before the deadline the match will be decided on a coin toss.

To find your team, search by pressing ctrl-F (or cmd-F on a mac) and enter the email of your team captain.

Those of you new to Cuppers may find it useful to take a quick look at the Cuppers Rules.

The 2010 tournament winners were SEH 10. (Harry Fisher, Tom Whiteley, Kate Wright and Jo Skeels)
